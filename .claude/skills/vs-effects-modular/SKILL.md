# Effects are modular (2026-11-02)

## Effect folders
- One folder per effect: `src/effects/<Name>/` holds its C++, `README.md` (what it does, inputs, settings, passes, edge cases,
  Vulkan path, what correct/wrong looks like), `defaults.toml` (its settings defaults) and `SHADERS.txt` (its shader files; the
  shaders themselves stay in `src/hlsl/NewVegas/`, mirroring the game's `Shaders\VegasShaders\` layout).
- Index: `src/effects/INDEX.md`, generated by `tools/effects_index.py`.
- **Read the effect's README.md before changing it, and update it in the same change.** A stale doc is worse than none.

## Settings defaults
- `resource\VegasShaders.defaults.toml` is what ships; a key missing from it is not read.
- Edit the fragment (`src/effects/<Name>/defaults.toml`, or `resource/defaults/<Name>.toml` / `Core.toml` for sections without
  an effect folder), then `py -3.10 -I tools/defaults_tool.py merge .`; `check` verifies the committed
  `resource/VegasShaders.defaults.toml` matches. A new section also needs its line in `resource/defaults/ORDER.txt`.
- The room's hook `.claude/hooks/defaults-check.ps1` runs `check` after an edit to a fragment or ORDER.txt.
- Changing a default that players may have saved needs a migration step in `Configuration::UpdateOldDefaults()`
  (`src\core\SettingManager.cpp`) and a higher settings version, because the menu's Save writes every key.

## Frame order and adding an effect
- Frame order: `src/core/EffectChain.cpp` (two ordered step tables, pre- and post-tonemap).
- Adding an effect = a step there, a member in `ShaderManager` (Effects struct + registration), its folder, its README.

## Effect constants
- `EffectRecord` binds an effect's `TESR_` parameters (constants and samplers) by DECLARATION ORDER in the .fx file; a
  `register()` on them is ignored (`src/core/EffectRecord.cpp`).
